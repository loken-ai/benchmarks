# Notices

## Recordings

`fixtures/asr/jfk.wav`, the recording the speech recognition cells transcribe, is an excerpt of
John F. Kennedy's inaugural address of 20 January 1961, a work of the United States federal
government in the public domain. The file is the 16 kHz mono sample shipped with whisper.cpp (MIT).

`fixtures/asr/librispeech-20/`, the clip set the audio-input check transcribes, is 20 utterances
of the LibriSpeech ASR corpus test-clean subset (Panayotov, Chen, Povey and Khudanpur, 2015), CC BY
4.0, from openslr.org/12: for each of the first 20 speakers by number, the first utterance by id
lasting 3 to 15 s, with its transcript, written as 16 kHz 16-bit mono WAV.
